## 🔍 Finding Terms by Topic

### PHYSICS

#### Unit 1: Motion, Forces, Energy
**Kinematics**: distance, displacement, speed, velocity, acceleration
**Graphs**: distance-time, displacement-time, velocity-time, speed-time
**Forces**: force, balanced forces, unbalanced forces, Newton's laws
**Momentum**: momentum, impulse, impulse-momentum theorem, conservation of momentum
**Energy**: work, kinetic energy, gravitational potential energy, elastic potential energy
**Power**: power, efficiency, hooke's law, spring constant, extension, compression, elastic limit

#### Unit 2: Particulate Nature of Matter
**Atoms**: atom, electron, proton, neutron, nucleus, atomic number, mass number
**Substance Scale**: molecule, ion, relative atomic mass, relative formula mass
**Mole Concept**: avogadro's constant, mole, molar mass, molar volume
**States**: solid, liquid, gas, crystalline structure, amorphous structure, density
**Phase Changes**: melting, freezing, evaporation, boiling, condensation, sublimation, deposition
**Thermodynamic Properties**: specific heat capacity, latent heat, heating curve, temperature
**Kinetic Theory**: kinetic theory of gases, particle collisions, boyle's law, charles's law
**Gas Laws**: pressure law, combined gas law, ideal gas equation, molar volume of gas
**Diffusion**: diffusion, effusion, graham's law
**Intermolecular Forces**: intermolecular force, van der waals force, london dispersion force
**Hydrogen Bonding**: hydrogen bond, dipole-dipole interaction, volatile substance

#### Unit 3: Thermal Energy (GENERATING)
**Energy Concepts**: internal energy, heat, first law of thermodynamics, work in thermodynamic
**Heat Transfer**: specific heat capacity (detailed), calorimetry, enthalpy, second law
**Entropy**: entropy, reversible reactions, irreversible reactions
**Processes**: exothermic, endothermic, heat engine

#### Unit 4: Waves and Sound (GENERATING)
**Wave Properties**: wave, wavelength, frequency, amplitude, period, wave velocity
**Wave Types**: transverse wave, longitudinal wave, compression, rarefaction
**Sound**: sound, ultrasound, infrasound, intensity, decibel scale
**Sound Properties**: pitch, loudness, timbre, doppler effect
**Wave Behavior**: reflection of waves, refraction of waves, refractive index, diffraction, interference
**Double-Slit**: double-slit interference, polarization

#### Unit 5: Electricity & Magnetism (GENERATING)
**Electrostatics**: electric charge, coulomb's law, electric field, electric field strength
**Potential**: electric potential, potential difference, electron volt, terminal voltage
**Current**: electric current, conventional current, ohm's law, resistance, resistivity
**Power**: power (electrical), kilowatt-hour, internal resistance
**Superconductivity**: superconductivity, magnetism concepts, magnetic fields

### CHEMISTRY

#### Unit 1: Chemical Change & Reactions
**Reactions**: chemical reaction, balancing equations, exothermic reaction, endothermic reaction
**Kinetics**: activation energy, catalyst, reaction rate, collision theory
**Rate Factors**: factors affecting reaction rate, temperature effects, concentration effects
**Equilibrium**: reversible reactions, irreversible reactions, equilibrium, equilibrium constant
**Le Chatelier**: le chatelier's principle, pressure effects, concentration effects, temperature effects
**Thermochemistry**: enthalpy, hess's law, bond energy, heat of reaction

#### Unit 2: Acids, Bases, Salts
**Definitions**: acid, base, strong acid, weak acid, strong base, weak base
**Quantification**: pH scale, acid dissociation constant, base dissociation constant
**Reactions**: neutralization, salt formation, hydrolysis
**Solutions**: buffer solution, indicator, titration, titration analysis

#### Unit 3: Bonding & Structure
**Bond Types**: chemical bond, covalent bond, ionic bond, metallic bond
**Properties**: electronegativity, bond polarity, molecular polarity
**Structures**: lewis structure, vsepr theory, crystal structures
**Properties**: hardness, melting points, solubility, electrical conductivity

#### Unit 4: Organic Chemistry (GENERATING)
**Hydrocarbons**: alkanes, alkenes, alkynes, arenes
**Functional Groups**: alcohols, carboxylic acids, esters, amines, amides
**Properties**: isomerism, nomenclature, reactions
**Polymers**: polymerization, addition polymers, condensation polymers

#### Unit 5: Periodicity & Groups (GENERATING)
**Trends**: atomic radius, ionization energy, electronegativity, metallic character
**Group 1**: alkali metals, properties, reactions, compounds
**Group 2**: alkaline earth metals, properties, reactions, compounds
**Group 17**: halogens, properties, reactions, displacement reactions
**Transition Metals**: d-block elements, variable oxidation states, complex ions

### BIOLOGY

#### Unit 1: Cell Structure (GENERATING)
**Prokaryotic vs Eukaryotic**: prokaryotic cell, eukaryotic cell, differences
**Organelles**: nucleus, mitochondria, chloroplast, endoplasmic reticulum, golgi apparatus
**Membranes**: cell membrane, nuclear envelope, membrane structure

#### Unit 2: Transport (GENERATING)
**Mechanisms**: diffusion, osmosis, active transport, facilitated diffusion
**Tissue Transport**: xylem, phloem, translocation

#### Unit 3: Photosynthesis (GENERATING)
**Reactions**: light-dependent reactions, light-independent reactions, calvin cycle
**Photosystems**: photosystem I, photosystem II, electron transport chain
**Products**: glucose, ATP, NADPH, water splitting

#### Unit 4: Respiration (GENERATING)
**Aerobic**: glycolysis, krebs cycle, electron transport chain, oxidative phosphorylation
**Anaerobic**: fermentation, lactate fermentation, ethanol fermentation
**Energy**: ATP, ADP, energy yield

#### Unit 5: Homeostasis & Coordination (GENERATING)
**Regulation**: osmoregulation, thermoregulation, negative feedback
**Organs**: kidney, ADH, urea cycle
**Nervous System**: neuron, synapse, reflex arc, nervous response
**Endocrine**: hormones, hormone regulation, insulin, glucagon

#### Unit 6: Genetics & DNA (GENERATING)
**DNA**: deoxyribonucleic acid, structure, replication, semi-conservative replication
**Gene Expression**: transcription, translation, protein synthesis, mutations
**Cell Division**: mitosis, cytokinesis, meiosis, reduction division
**Inheritance**: genes, alleles, dominant, recessive, genetic crosses

#### Unit 7: Ecology (GENERATING)
**Ecosystems**: biotic, abiotic, food chain, food web
**Populations**: population growth, carrying capacity, competition, predation
**Biodiversity**: speciation, evolution, natural selection, conservation

**Applications**: optimization, rates of change
