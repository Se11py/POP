# IGCSE Mathematics (0580) & A Level Flashcards

## [IGCSE] UNIT 1: NUMBER
- **Front**: Prime Number
  **Back**: A number greater than 1 with exactly two distinct factors: 1 and itself.
- **Front**: Rational Number
  **Back**: A number that can be expressed as a fraction a/b where a and b are integers and b ≠ 0.
- **Front**: Irrational Number
  **Back**: A number that cannot be written as a simple fraction; its decimal expansion is non-terminating and non-recurring (e.g., π, √2).
- **Front**: Standard Form (Scientific Notation)
  **Back**: A way of writing numbers as A x 10^n where 1 ≤ A < 10 and n is an integer.
- **Front**: Upper and Lower Bounds
  **Back**: The maximum and minimum possible values of a rounded number (± half the unit of accuracy).
- **Front**: Simple Interest Formula
  **Back**: I = (P * R * T) / 100.
- **Front**: Compound Interest Formula
  **Back**: A = P(1 + r/100)^n.
- **Front**: Recurring Decimal to Fraction
  **Back**: Let x = decimal, multiply by 10/100/1000 to align decimals, subtract to eliminate recurring part, and solve for x.

## [IGCSE] UNIT 2: ALGEBRA & GRAPHS
- **Front**: Difference of Two Squares
  **Back**: a² - b² = (a - b)(a + b).
- **Front**: Quadratic Formula
  **Back**: x = [-b ± √(b² - 4ac)] / 2a.
- **Front**: Discriminant (b² - 4ac)
  **Back**: Used to find the number of roots: >0 (2 real roots), =0 (1 repeated root), <0 (no real roots).
- **Front**: Gradient of a Straight Line (m)
  **Back**: m = (y₂ - y₁) / (x₂ - x₁).
- **Front**: Perpendicular Gradients
  **Back**: The product of perpendicular gradients is -1 (m₁ * m₂ = -1).
- **Front**: Function Notation f(x)
  **Back**: Represents a relationship where every input x has exactly one output y.
- **Front**: Inverse Function f⁻¹(x)
  **Back**: The function that reverses the effect of f(x); found by swapping x and y and solving for y.
- **Front**: Composite Function fg(x)
  **Back**: The output of g(x) is used as the input for f(x).

## [IGCSE] UNIT 3: GEOMETRY & TRIGONOMETRY
- **Front**: Sine Rule
  **Back**: a/sinA = b/sinB = c/sinC (used for non-right-angled triangles).
- **Front**: Cosine Rule (for side)
  **Back**: a² = b² + c² - 2bc cosA.
- **Front**: Cosine Rule (for angle)
  **Back**: cosA = (b² + c² - a²) / 2bc.
- **Front**: Area of a Triangle (Non-right-angled)
  **Back**: Area = ½ab sinC.
- **Front**: Interior Angle of a Regular Polygon
  **Back**: Angle = (n - 2) * 180 / n.
- **Front**: Circle Theorem: Angle at Center
  **Back**: The angle at the center is twice the angle at the circumference subtended by the same arc.
- **Front**: Circle Theorem: Opposite Angles in Cyclic Quadrilateral
  **Back**: Opposite angles in a cyclic quadrilateral add up to 180°.
- **Front**: Bearings
  **Back**: Measured clockwise from North, always expressed as three digits (e.g., 045°).

## [IGCSE] UNIT 4: STATISTICS & PROBABILITY
- **Front**: Interquartile Range (IQR)
  **Back**: Upper Quartile (Q3) - Lower Quartile (Q1).
- **Front**: Mean from Frequency Table
  **Back**: Σ(fx) / Σf.
- **Front**: Probability of Mutually Exclusive Events
  **Back**: P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B).
- **Front**: Probability of Independent Events
  **Back**: P(A and B) = P(A) * P(B).

## [A LEVEL] CORE FUNDAMENTALS
- **Front**: Logarithm Definition
  **Back**: If y = a^x, then x = log_a(y).
- **Front**: Differentiation of x^n
  **Back**: d/dx(x^n) = nx^(n-1).
- **Front**: Integration of x^n
  **Back**: ∫x^n dx = (x^(n+1) / (n+1)) + C.
- **Front**: Radians to Degrees
  **Back**: π radians = 180°. To convert degrees to radians, multiply by π/180.
